§ 02 — The concierge · La concierge

If a chair is easier
than a form, write us.Si le fauteuil est plus simple — écrivez.

Élise, our house concierge, sits at the door from 09:00 to 22:00. She speaks French and English, holds appointments at her desk on a paper book, and will thread you to the artist most for the hour you're imagining.

Open her at the bottom-right of any page — or simply call. We answer the phone before the second ring; that's the only thing we time at this address.
